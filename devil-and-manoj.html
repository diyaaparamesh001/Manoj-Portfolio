Devil and Manoj

By Manoj Ashodia

Manoj arrives in hell. A bored Devil guards the door.

The Devil "Okay. So here you are finally. I need some answers."

Manoj "Why?"

The Devil "To check if you're compatible here."

Manoj "Yeah. Shoot."

The Devil "Your favorite song?"

Manoj "California Dreamin'."

The Devil "Your actual favorite song?"

Manoj "Hips don't lie."

The Devil "That's neat."

Manoj "Thank you."

The Devil "Your favorite film?"

Manoj "2046."

The Devil "Again…"

Manoj "The Shawshank Redemption."

The Devil "Your favorite person?"

Manoj "You!"

The Devil (rolls his eyes) "Do we need to…"

Manoj "Okay. The person I once met on a lonely train. Who looked at me as if she knew me. But she didn't. Because I didn't."

The Devil "Is it possible she knew you actually?"

Manoj "Yes. But I didn't."

The Devil "She must be wondering if you looked like her lover she had moved on from years ago. Did you think about it?"

Manoj "Perhaps."

The Devil "Perhaps what?"

Manoj "Perhaps I reminded her of someone."

The Devil "How did you respond?"

Manoj "I told her we all die. And I walked away."

The Devil "How in the world is she your favorite person then?"

Manoj "In those few moments, she had an unwavering focus on me."

The Devil "And you?"

Manoj "I was gloomy, thinking about a person who knew me for years but decided to leave me one day."

The Devil "Why?"

Manoj "I loved too much."

The Devil "No, why did you think of her suddenly in that moment?"

Manoj "Because, my mind my feelings my rules."

The Devil "What about this new girl?"

Manoj "She loved me more than I loved her."

The Devil "Such pity."

Manoj "Anything else?"

The Devil "What's your favorite food?"

Manoj "Chicken biryani."

The Devil "Muradabadi?"

Manoj "Hyderab..."

Devil (shouts) "TAKE THIS MAN AWAY AND BURN HIM IN ETERNAL FIRE."

Manoj "A'ight. But do they serve choley bhature in there?"

Devil "Yeah."

Manoj "Okay. Bring that eternal fire."

Devil brought the eternal fire. Manoj took a warm bath and ate a good plate of chholey bhature without lassi.

Apparently, that's the reason he was in hell. Because time, as they say in hell, moves in circles.
