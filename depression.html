Mental Health: Depression

By Manoj Ashodia · A Ghost and a Human

"Hey Human. Are you there?"

"Yes, Ghost. Tell me, what now?"

"What is depression?"

"I am not sure?"

"What do you mean? You are a human and aren't you guys pretty accustomed to feeling depress..?"

"I know the feeling. But I don't know if that's the right word to express that feeling when you are…"

"What? Depressed"

"Would you stop using this word? It is hurting my brain?"

"Okay. But what exactly is the feeling of being… you know I am talking about?"

"Its not that easy to describe either. It works differently for everyone."
