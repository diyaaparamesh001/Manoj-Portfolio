About Me

Manoj Ashodia is an Indian writer, screenwriter and a filmmaker.

He came forth into the world on a rainy August morning, which he largely deems to be a tragedy. And hence, his lifelong hatred of rains, among other things. He learned early on that he was good with words and could channel his woes in proses and poetry. And later on he also learned that his words sell good. So he became a professional writer. He wrote all things that are important, and then some.

He began writing ad copies in his early 20s, along with articles and blogs on travel, tech, cinema, TV, fintech, mythology and everything under the sun, except, ironically, the sun. He was always a cinema aficionado, which led him to write screenplays and advertisement scripts. When that didn't suffice, he himself made some films, that were deemed good enough to be picked up to stream by platforms such as Mx Player, JioHotstar and Stage OTT.

He built a small fortune owing to these creative adventures and would like you to contribute to it in your own capacity.

Plus, he's a morning person and most creative 3 hours post waking up. So get him to write between 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM IST and you'll get the best worth out of your money.
