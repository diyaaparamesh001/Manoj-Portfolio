A Layman's View of Hatred

By Manoj Ashodia · New Delhi

I was reading Fyodor Dostoevsky's Russian classic 'The Idiot' and there is a quote that stuck with me- 'Beauty will save the world'. Tucked safely in my large room of my upper-middle-class household, in a safe locality, belonging to a 'fearsome' Haryanavi community, I highlighted the quote and shut my eyes. What does it mean, I wondered. Russian society was torn between Conservatism and Liberalism in the second half of the 1800s, and Dostoevsky had some thoughts on it, but never a conclusive statement on whether he was in favour or against any ideology.. It was evident in almost all his books, post his return from Siberia. I wondered, was he aware that the world would be needing this statement a century and a half later, in the country that would become one of Russia's closest allies in the coming century.

I walked out of my room to ask my mom if the dinner was ready, only to find her glued to the TV. The news broadcaster seemed too elated about the recent arrest of Delhi's Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal. I had heard the news in passing, in random discussions in public places and some social media posts here and there. I was aware of it, but I did not care about it. Okay, it happened. He must be guilty, or not, time will tell. But no big deal, politicians go to jail all the time. But what alarmed me was the fact that the journalist, who seemed like a decently educated woman, was excited about it as if she personally abhorred Arvind Kejriwal. And as I sat with my parents and slowly consumed what was being said, I began to get anxious, and my anxiety increased by the moment I witnessed the death of journalism right in front of me, on live TV.

'Sahi hua, chor sala (it's good, bloody theif)', remarked my dad. I questioned him what did he know about it? He told me his version of it, and as expected, it was exactly what the news broadcaster was rambling about. It further caused me a lot of concern. Being a journalist student myself, I know they aren't always reliable, and they are certainly not supposed to be 'emotional' about a piece of news of information. That takes away the objectivity out of it, which is what news and information is all about. I didn't argue with my dad, I just left the room, and shut myself back in my room and ended up researching about the entire thing on my own.

I have been inactive about the whole political humbug happening in my country for years now. Since, I knew for sure we are moving towards an electoral autocracy and that no one gives a shit anymore. I shut myself off completely from electoral politics. BJP will do anything to win the elections and Congress is meek and AAP is still a mere ant to BJP's elephant. There was no stopping the behemoth led by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, so why bother?

But then again, why shouldn't I bother? Why shouldn't I be worried when my Muslim best friend can soon be classified as a second class citizen? Why shouldn't I bother when my recently deceased uncle's son who thought he could do farming for a living is not even breaking even, let alone making a living out of producing eatables that keep us all alive. Why shouldn't I be bothered when I see religious pride being the dominant thought in a voter's head. And why shouldn't I be bothered when my own relatives and closest friends are severely brainwashed. I would bother. This is my country too. I have been proud of it for too long, only to see it losing its secular, liberal, peaceful identity? No. I have been proud of India because I was born here and most probably, I would die here.

I was shaken to my core when I saw that notorious Manipur video of a woman paraded naked by a mob and thought that humanity couldn't stoop any lower. But when I came to know that our honourable Prime Minister didn't even visit the place and let it burn, it gave me nightmares.

Majority wins, everywhere. In the U.S., blacks are oppressed, in Pakistan, Hindus are second class citizen, and in India, Muslims are the new 'common enemy'. When I got into a little bit more into the whole shebang going on in form of news on national channels, I was stunned, and shocked. What do they seek? Civil war? A Muslim genocide in the name of what, Ethnic Cleansing?

Their children study abroad, in the best universities, and then, who would shed blood for the sake of thier politics? YOU! My 15 years old cousin with a Jai Shree Ram sticker on his bike and unreasonable or unjustified hatred for an entire community in his heart. He will fight their battles. He will kill and die. He is the one, who'll ruin his entire life! And I am a laymen, I have no greater motive, no affiliation to any political party, or no side to take, I am scared for my family. I am scared that when the 'common enemy' is eliminated, a new common enemy will be built from scratch and the cycle will repeat all over again. Until, this country is left to burn at the hands of a group of illiterate power-hungry monarchs that call them 'publicly elected representatives.'

What would they be representing? Think again.
