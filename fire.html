Fire

By Manoj Ashodia

At first, it was a wildfire that burned the entire forest down, taking my house down with it. In the evaporated disarray that pulled its way up–because nothing ever dies, and nothing ever lives forever–a cruel joke was played on me. I tried to look for myself in the smoke, hoping the fire had died for good, but it didn't.

It was very much alive, only this time in parts on a small blade of grass hiding behind a gigantic marble statue I built of my love. Love once ignited, never dies, it conveyed to me. I sprinkled an ocean on the fire, and yet, it still found a way to burn subtly and evaporate the remnants of my house.

Such passion was the love I had in my heart for her that I was too afraid to let my house burn, because she had once touched its floor, and furniture, and the bedsheet. Every time I tried to go near the flames, however big or small, I always emerged with a small burn scar, and that scar itched on my body like a fresh tattoo, reminding me of a simpler time when love was just 'you take my sadness I take yours' and nothing more, nothing less. There was no burning passion involved, just existing in each other's calming presence. Just lying naked in the bed, in the moment, experiencing tender bodily warmth, the subtle aroma of cinnamon oil, and ginger tea being boiled in the kitchen.

My love isn't totally unrequited, though as it seems. I claimed it in tiny spurts. An accidental touch of hands, a warm friendly hug, laughter shared, a stare into my eyes, sharing a spoon, and even a glimpse of her in my dream. I had stored enough water from these moments to tackle the impending wildfire. But as it turned out, it wasn't enough.

And actually, I have less faith in the poets when they say- that love only lasts until it isn't realized. I believe the fire never dies in the forest until the forest is completely turned into ashes.

In her jungle, there is no fire, or there is a fire that she is good at masking.

Next time I meet her, if I survive this fire, I'll look for the smoke in her jungle.
